# B1 / B2 methodology v5 („lesson budget”) — the school methodologist's decisions, 2026-11-25

Normative for the Estonian courses B1 (A2→B1, `a2b1-*`) and B2 (B1→B2, `b1b2-*`). Where this document and older
rules disagree (`CEFR_A2_B1_LEARNING_STANDARD.md` §2 „7–9 blocks per sheet” and §13 „≥ 25 min of closed drills”,
`CODEX_TASKS_B1B2_IDEAL.md` „keep every old item, 40–55 min per sheet”), **this document wins** for every module listed
in `strictModules.json`. The evidence behind each rule is in `docs/review/B1-B2-plaan-2026-11-25.md` and the
pedagogical audit of 2026-11-25.

Owner's mandate (2026-11-25): „Сделай так, чтобы это принесло ученикам максимально эффективно и полезно, учитывая
дидактику, современную педагогику, коннективизм … веди себя как методист школы”. His complaint: too much that is
unnecessary, too little that is needed, tasks often not at the level.

## 1. What a lesson is for

One lesson = **one real communicative outcome** the learner can do afterwards and could not do before (CEFR Companion
Volume 2020, action-oriented approach). Everything on the sheet either builds towards that outcome or is cut. „Ma
oskan …” at the end names that outcome in the learner's words.

## 2. Principles (why the template looks as it does)

1. **Task first, form in service of the task (TBLT).** Pre-task (meet the situation and the input) → task → focus on
   form (the language the task needed) → the task again, changed (repetition with variation). Grammar is never a lesson
   goal on its own in B1/B2; it is the tool for the outcome.
2. **Cognitive load.** One input per lesson. At most 15 blocks (B1) / 14 (B2) for the whole lesson, a 75-minute core.
   Worked example → guided → independent: support fades inside the lesson and disappears by the module's end.
3. **Retrieval, spacing, interleaving.** Recall without looking (warm-up from the last lesson, not a re-read); drills of
   words and forms live in **Harjuta** (spaced repetition, Duolingo-like), not on the lesson sheet. Each module opens
   with a short *cold task* and closes with a *parallel new task* — growth is measured on transfer, not on the same
   exercise repeated.
4. **Formative assessment.** Success criteria visible before the task (3 points). Feedback leads to an obligatory
   correction (own sentence corrected, not only marked). Writing at B2 and the module task at B1 use an **analytic
   rubric** with level descriptors and a borderline sample; a checklist is for self-editing, not for grading.
5. **Independence is evidence.** Every module has one speaking and one writing task **without ready phrases and without
   a given opening**, and one role card where the teacher holds hidden information or an unexpected obstacle (the
   learner must clarify, react, solve) — as in b1b2-006.
6. **Connectivism: learning through real networks.** Each module has one *link task* that connects the learner with
   real Estonian-language sources and people: find and use a real source (ERR news, eesti.ee, a municipality / library
   / shop page, a timetable, a real ad), write or call a real service or a classmate, share the product with the group
   and answer one peer. The sheet links to the source (title + URL) and never copies it. The learner keeps a small
   personal network: one podcast / channel / page in Estonian they follow and report on once a module.
7. **Autonomy and relevance.** A choice in every Kasuta (topic, role or addressee). Topics follow Harno's broad themes
   with real facts about Estonia; the continuing story (Sofia, Markus, …) appears in at most one lesson per module.
8. **Differentiation.** Core vs. „Kui aega jääb” is a teacher-plan matter, not extra blocks on the sheet. A weaker
   learner gets more support in the same task, not a different, easier task.
9. **Exam literacy without teaching to the test.** Harno formats are trained inside real tasks; full mock exams only at
   the planned points. Thresholds are named one way everywhere: *Harno: pass from 60 %. KeeleSepp: ready for the exam
   from 70 %, our certificate from 80 % (internal threshold).*
10. **No dictation in B1–C1** (owner 2026-11-04). Pair speaking in the lesson, voice recording at home.

## 3. Lesson budget (G1–G3)

| | B1 | B2 |
|---|---|---|
| Blocks per sheet (Avasta / Harjuta / Kasuta) | ≤ 5 / ≤ 4 / ≤ 5 | ≤ 4 / ≤ 4 / ≤ 5 |
| Blocks per lesson | ≤ 15 | ≤ 14 |
| Core time | 75 min (5 + 15 + 20 + 25 + 5 + buffer 15) | 75 min (5 + 20 + 20 + 25 + 5) |
| Input | one text or one recording | alternating: reading 400–600 words (≥ 2 inference questions) or listening 3–4 min (8–12 items, 2 voices) |
| Harjuta (sheet) | notice → rule → one form task 8–10 items → one task „write a whole sentence” | rule + two tasks of 8–10 items **without a word bank** + one register / paraphrase task |
| Kasuta | the module's outcome task (pair; 2nd attempt without phrases) + exit (3 points + „Ma oskan”) | debate ≤ 4 min with cards or 1-min monologue after 2 min preparation + argument plan + exit |
| Home | one letter, 1 min voice, words in Harjuta | one letter 140 or 180 words with the analytic rubric, 1 min voice |

Lengths with a floor **and a ceiling** (levelStage by lesson number):

| B1 lessons | text | audio | writing | speech |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 001–020 (A2) | 80–150 words | ≤ 1.5 min | 30–60 words | 30–60 s |
| 021–040 (A2+) | 120–180 | ≤ 2 min | 50–80 | 60–90 s |
| 041–060 (A2+/B1-) | 150–220 | ≤ 2.5 min | 60–90 | 90–120 s |
| 061–090 (B1) | 180–300 | ≤ 3.5 min | ~50 + ~100 | 120–180 s |

B2: reading 400–600 (control up to 600), listening 3–4 min (clips 35–45 s heard once), writing ~140 + ~180, monologue
1 min after 2 min preparation, discussion ≤ 4 min.

## 4. What leaves the lesson sheet

- **To Harjuta** (practice in the student cabinet): crosswords, matching, sorting, word order, word cards, „Mäletad?”,
  any drill beyond 10 items. From B1 lesson 041 on and in all of B2 these block types are not on lesson sheets at all.
- **Cut:** exam rubrics in ordinary lessons (kept in controls; one criteria card per module), „Kas teadsid?” unless the
  task uses it, the 4-3-2 triple monologue, „Parem versioon” in class (it is the homework correction), the second and
  third input text, the A2 opener text, a second and third speaking card, three writing tasks in one lesson.
- Removed blocks are **hidden, not deleted** (ids and learners' answers stay).

## 5. Controls and exams

- Module control: **one sheet, 45 min, ≤ 10 blocks**, two skills alternating; raw points, Harno 60 % and the internal
  70 / 80 % shown.
- Full mock exams only at B1 060 / 080 / 089 / 090 and B2 020 / 040 / 060 / 080 / 089 / 090.
- 9th-grade e-exam lessons (`a2b1-ee*`) leave the adult B1 path: they belong to the school track (`ek9`), where they are
  offered to pupils only.

## 6. Gates (tests) that enforce this

G1 block budget · G2 no „40–55 min per sheet” / „≥ 25 min drills” for strict modules · G3 lengths with ceilings · G4 B2
input rule (≥ 400-word reading with inference or ≥ 3-min listening; gaps without a bank) · G5 no dictation (exists) ·
G6 audio: play count set, 2× (B2 clips 1×), lock in controls · G7 story cast in ≤ 1 lesson per module · G8 decoration
(≤ 1 tip per lesson, `examrubric` only in controls, no crossword / match / order on lesson sheets from B1 041 and in B2)
· G9 independence (one speech + one writing without phrases, one hidden-information card per module) · G10 B2 writing
rubric (4 criteria with descriptors) · G11 thresholds named · G12 no „pausideta” in A2/B1 rubrics · G13 link task:
each module has one block with a real source link (title + https URL) or a real addressee.

Gates run per module from `strictModulesV5.json` so that a module is switched on when it is rewritten.

### 6.1 How the gates are built (2026-11-25, branch agent/b1b2-gates-v5)

- **List:** `crm-v2/src/features/worksheet-generator/admin/course/strictModulesV5.json` — `{ "b1": [module ids], "b2": [...] }`,
  empty at first. The older `strictModules.json` is a different gate (every quality gate, almost all modules listed) and
  is not changed. Switch a module on by adding its `MODULE.id` (e.g. `"a2b1-module-01"`) to its course list.
- **Code:** `admin/course/methodV5.js` (pure, node-importable): `methodV5Input(mod)`, `methodV5Problems(input)` →
  `[{ gate, code, lessonId, phase, blockId, text }]`, `methodV5Report(mod)`, `formatMethodV5Report(report)`. Test:
  `admin/course/methodV5.test.js` (every listed module has zero problems + a synthetic module with one violation per gate).
- **Report before switching:** `node tools/course-audit/method-v5.mjs --course b1 --module 1` (or `--course b2 --module 17`;
  `--gate G1,G3`; `--json`; without `--module` the counts per gate of every module). From crm-v2: `npm run course:method-v5 -- …`.
- **G2:** the sheets of a listed module carry `meta.methodV5: true` (`courseSheet.js isMethodV5`); `quality.js` then skips the
  6–18 / 5–9 block count and the 40–55 min per sheet, `depth.js` skips „≥ 25 min of closed drills” (`thin`, `live-fast`), the
  ≥ 2 whole-sentence tasks per sheet, and counts 8 items as a full form task (exit checks exempt).
- **Hidden blocks:** `hide(block, why)` (blocks.js) sets `hidden: true`; `courseSheet.js` leaves the block off every sheet (a
  phase whose blocks are all hidden is no sheet), so it is in no gate and no count, but its id stays in the module file.
- **What counts:** every block on the sheet except the textbook picture (`data.artId` / `data.artFor`), word strips and hidden
  blocks. Lesson kinds: teaching lesson; module control (`assessment`); mock exam (an assessment at B1 060/080/089/090, B2
  020/040/060/080/089/090 — no budget, no length ceilings); oral / theory / diagnostic / e-exam sheets (only G5–G7, G12).
- **Markers the gates read:** hidden-information card = a `rolecards` / `speakcard` whose teacher note (`teach()`) contains
  „Varjatud info:” or `data.hiddenInfo: true` (G9); free speech = `speaking` without `tipText` on a sheet without `phrasebank`;
  free writing = `writing` without `keywords` or a letter / `examwriting` without `opening` (G9); link task = a block with a
  title / heading and an `https://` URL in its learner text, or `data.realAddressee` (G13); analytic rubric = `examrubric` /
  `rubric` with ≥ 4 `criteria`, each with a `hint` or `levels` (G10; such a rubric may stand in a B2 lesson with a ≥ 120-word
  writing task, every other `examrubric` only in controls, G8); cast = `CAST` in methodV5.js (names from COURSE_CAST.md, case
  forms counted, „Anna mulle …” not).
- **Estimates:** audio length = the recorded file's seconds (`listeningAudio.json`) or words ÷ 128 per minute; a `writing`
  block's words = sentences × 8; a pair card (`speakcard`) in B1 may run twice the stage's speech ceiling; lesson time =
  the sheets' `timeEstimate.js` minutes, ceiling 90. B2 inference questions are found by words (miks, autor, vihjab, arvab,
  järeldus …) in the reading's questions and the sheet's choice questions; the two voices of a B2 recording are not checked.
