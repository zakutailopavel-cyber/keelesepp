# Eesti keel 5–7-aastastele: grupitundide plaan ja lastetunni formaat

Owner 2026-10-26: „планы для дошкольников и организация групп для проведения групповых уроков … план для групповых
уроков 5–7 лет, как подготовишка, но тут нашего генератора явно не хватит, нужен детский формат”.

This document is the pedagogical contract for the preschool group course („Mudilaste eesti keel”). The worksheet
generator (Avasta / Harjuta / Kasuta) is for readers: children of 5–7 mostly do not read yet. A lesson for them is a
**sequence of activities with picture cards, movement and songs**, not a worksheet. Status: a proposal for the owner's
approval. The CRM part (§7) is not built yet.

## 1. Who and how

| | |
|---|---|
| Age | 5–7 years (the year before school and the 1st class). Mixed 5 + 7 works when the group is small |
| Starting point | zero or very little Estonian; Russian-, Ukrainian- or English-speaking homes |
| Group | 4–6 children (at most 8 with an assistant). In the CRM: a group with Sihtrühm „Eelkooliealised (5–7 a)”, 6 seats |
| Length | 45 minutes, once or twice a week. A 5-year-old focuses for 5–8 minutes per activity, so a lesson has 7–8 short parts |
| Room | on site: a free floor (a carpet circle) + a table. Online works only for 6–7-year-olds, 30 min, groups of 2–3, with a parent nearby |
| Year | 32 weeks = 8 blocks × 4 weeks (Sept–May), plus holiday lessons |
| Goal | by the end of the year a child understands simple instructions and short stories with pictures, and can say who they are, what they like and what they see (≈ 300 understood words, ≈ 150 spoken). No reading or writing goals; letters only as play in block 8 |

The teacher speaks Estonian in the lesson (gestures, pictures, mime). The home language is used only to comfort a child
or to explain a rule of a game, never to translate the words.

## 2. The structure of one lesson (45 min)

| # | Part | Min | What happens | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Tere-ring** (greeting circle) | 4 | The same greeting song each time, names, „Kuidas sul läheb?” with emotion cards | ritual and safety; the same phrases 32 times |
| 2 | **Kordamine** (review) | 5 | Last week's picture cards: „Mis see on?”, a quick game (Mis on kadunud? — one card is hidden) | spaced repetition |
| 3 | **Uued sõnad** (new words) | 7 | 6–8 picture cards or real objects; the teacher shows → the children repeat in chorus → one by one; TPR („näita”, „too”, „anna”) | input before output |
| 4 | **Liikumismäng** (movement game) | 6 | the new words in a game: „Jookse punase juurde!”, „Saja sõna hüpe”, statues, the hot potato | 5-year-olds learn with their bodies; energy out |
| 5 | **Laul või luuletus** (song / rhyme) | 5 | one song per block with gestures; Estonian children's folk songs and rhymes the school is allowed to use | chunks and pronunciation |
| 6 | **Laua ülesanne** (table task) | 8 | colour, cut, glue, draw, a picture lotto or a big printed sheet (§4); the teacher talks while they work („Mis värvi see on?”) | fine motor skills; quiet time; one-to-one talk |
| 7 | **Lugu** (picture story) | 6 | a short picture story or a puppet scene with the block's words; questions with pointing („Kus on kass?”) | listening to longer speech |
| 8 | **Head aega-ring** | 4 | sticker / stamp, „Mis meile täna meeldis?”, the goodbye song; the take-home card goes into the bag | closure; the parent gets the words |

Rules: no part longer than 8 minutes; a quiet part after every active one; the same opening and closing every time;
every child speaks alone at least three times per lesson; praise for trying, not for correctness.

## 3. The year's plan (8 blocks × 4 weeks)

Each block has 24–30 words, 6–8 phrases, one song and one story. Week 4 of a block is the review and „show and tell”
week (the parents may watch for the last 10 minutes).

| Block | Theme | Words (examples) | Phrases | Song / rhyme | Story / game |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Sept | **Tere! Mina ja minu rühm** | tere, head aega, poiss, tüdruk, sõber, õpetaja, jah, ei, 1–5 | Minu nimi on … · Ma olen … aastat vana · Kuidas sul läheb? Hästi! | a greeting song | „Kes on kes?” name game; lotto of faces |
| 2 Oct | **Värvid ja sügis** | punane, kollane, sinine, roheline, … , leht, õun, seen, vihm | See on punane · Mis värvi? · Mulle meeldib … | an autumn song with movements | leaf colours; „Jookse … juurde” |
| 3 Nov | **Minu pere ja kodu** | ema, isa, vend, õde, vanaema, vanaisa, beebi, maja, tuba, voodi | See on minu ema · Mul on vend · Kus on …? | a family finger rhyme | a family puppet show; draw your family |
| 4 Dec | **Talv ja jõulud** | lumi, külm, müts, sall, kindad, kuusk, päkapikk, kink, jõuluvana | Mul on külm · Pane müts pähe · Häid jõule! | a Christmas / päkapikk song | dress the doll for winter; a päkapikk letter |
| 5 Jan | **Mänguasjad ja numbrid** | pall, nukk, auto, klotsid, karu, 6–10, suur, väike | Mul on kaks palli · Anna mulle … · Palun · Aitäh | a counting rhyme | a toy shop role play; „Mitu?” |
| 6 Feb | **Keha ja riided** | pea, käsi, jalg, silmad, nina, suu, kõht, särk, püksid, saapad | Näita oma ninakest! · Mul on valus … · Ma panen … selga | a body-parts action song | Simon says („Saja ütleb”); dress-up relay |
| 7 Mar–Apr | **Loomad ja kevad** | kass, koer, lehm, hobune, lind, jänes, karu, rebane, lill, päike | Kass ütleb mjäu · Kes elab metsas? · Ma oskan hüpata | an animal-sounds song | farm / forest sorting; animal mime |
| 8 May | **Toit, mängud õues ja „varsti kooli”** | leib, piim, supp, jäätis, mängima, jooksma, hüppama, A–O as picture-letters | Mulle maitseb … · Lähme õue! · Ma lähen kooli | a picnic / summer song | a picnic role play; letter-hunt with pictures (play only) |

Holiday lessons (one each): vastlapäev (sledging words), Halloween is not used, Eesti Vabariigi aastapäev (lipp, sinine-must-valge),
lihavõtted (muna, jänes), emadepäev (a card for mum).

## 4. What the generator can and cannot do for this course

- **Cannot (and should not):** reading tasks, gap-fill, word order, text answers, grammar explanations, auto-checked
  sheets. A child of 5 cannot read the instruction.
- **Can be reused:** the picture library (`textbook-art`, the art bible's black line + one lime accent suits colouring
  pages), Live Classroom's board for online 6–7-year-olds (drag a picture onto the right place, circle), printing.
- **New, child-format „lesson card” (proposal, §7):** one A4 for the teacher per lesson: the 8 parts with minutes,
  the cards to prepare, the game rules, the song, the questions for the story. Plus 2–3 A4 **picture sheets** for the
  table task: colour by word, match picture to picture, „find and circle”, cut-and-glue, picture lotto — every
  instruction is a pictogram plus one short Estonian sentence the teacher reads aloud.

## 5. How progress is seen (no tests)

Per block the teacher ticks a short observation list (in the CRM as „Ma oskan …” goals, the same evidence model as
the older learners, docs/ADAPTIVE_LESSON_SYSTEM.md), e.g. block 2:

- näitab sõna peale õiget värvi (understands 8/10 colours);
- ütleb pildi kohta „See on punane …” (says 5 colours);
- laulab laulu kaasa liigutustega;
- täidab korralduse „Jookse / too / näita …”.

Three marks: **Oskab** / **Harjutame** / **Veel mitte**. The parent sees the ticks and the take-home card, not
scores. A parent report is sent only via the admin button (owner rule).

## 6. Organising the groups

1. **Sign-up:** the website enquiry form (adult = no, the child's age) → Päringud. The level test (`/tasemetest`) is
   not used for under-7s; the teacher meets the child for a 10-minute play trial instead.
2. **Group:** Grupid → Lisa grupp → Sihtrühm „Eelkooliealised (5–7 a)” (6 seats, ages 5–7 are filled in) → time,
   e.g. Tue + Thu 17:00 or Sat 10:00 (the parents' times from „Sobivad ajad”).
3. **Filling:** „Täida” on the group card lists the children of the right age with the group time free, and the
   website enquiries with children (PR #829). Start the group at 4 children; open a waiting list beyond 6.
4. **Parents:** a short welcome letter (what to bring: indoor shoes, a folder for the cards; that the lesson is in
   Estonian; that the last 10 minutes of week 4 are open). Sent by the admin.
5. **Price:** per child per month (group price), billed like the other groups; a missed group lesson is not moved
   (the group goes on), unlike an individual lesson.
6. **Teacher:** one teacher with experience with small children; with 7–8 children an assistant.
