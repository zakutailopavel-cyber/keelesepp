# Minikursus „Käänded lauses” (course key `kaanded`)

Owner 2026-11-28: „сделай мини курс на тему склонений слов в предложении, чтобы научить учеников как это работает, с
примерами, схемами и заданиями на закрепление”. Six lessons for Russian-speaking learners at A2–B1, teens and adults. It is
a supplement (`MODULE.supplement: true`) that a teacher assigns by hand next to any level course. It is not a roadmap
module and is not in the strict course gates (v5 / v6 / final shape / academic value).

  - `002-sihitis`: the decision scheme for the object.
  - `003-sees`: the inner cases.
  - `004-peal`: the outer cases, people, having and time.

## Lessons

| # | Id | Lesson | Scheme | Communicative task |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | kaanded-001 | Mis on kääne ja milleks? | 14 cases, omastav + lõpp | pair interview (kus? kust? millega? kellega?) |
| 2 | kaanded-002 | Nimetav, omastav, osastav lauses | decision scheme täis- / osasihitis | role cards: the fridge and the shopping list |
| 3 | kaanded-003 | Sisekohakäänded: kuhu? kus? kust? | 3 houses + 3 × 3 grid | picture info gap (the room; B draws) |
| 4 | kaanded-004 | Väliskohakäänded: peal, inimene, aeg | 3 tables + uses, sees vs peal | town map: my day's route |
| 5 | kaanded-005 | Viimased viis käänet (-ks, -ni, -na, -ta, -ga) | 5 cards | pair interview: work and dreams |
| 6 | kaanded-006 | Kääne sõltub verbist. Kordamine ja kontroll | verb → case | survey „leia keegi, kes …” + control 30 p |

Every lesson has the same three sheets:

- **Avasta:**
  - „Mäletad?” (from lesson 2 on, with items from earlier lessons);
  - the scheme picture with two lines of text;
  - „Märka!”, the rule box with **bold** forms and a Russian contrast line;
  - a table or grid with the forms (gaps);
  - a short original text with questions;
  - a noticing task (sort, match, flow diagram or table).
- **Harjuta:**
  - the form text (base form in brackets, never the answer; the answers are the rule box's forms);
  - a choice task (3–4 options, side by side);
  - a transformation or error fixing task (typical errors of Russian speakers);
  - „Abi ja lisaväljakutse” (support line + stretch line).
- **Kasuta:**
  - the communicative task, with `taskType`, `support` (Abikaart) and `stretch` (Lisaväljakutse);
  - „Kirjuta oma laused”;
  - the exit check: 30 s of speech or 2 own sentences, plus 2 form gaps (`exit: true`);
  - the self-check with 3 criteria 0–1–2 and one own sentence as evidence;
  - the homework („Harjuta → Käänded lauses, tund N”).

In lesson 6, the control comes between „Kirjuta oma laused” and the exit check. It has four parts, one point per item:
choice 8, form text 10, transformation 6, error fixing 6 = **30 p**. The thresholds are 27 / 21 / 15. A test checks that
a full key scores 30 / 30.

Answer keys: every gap, table cell and word form was checked with Vabamorf (EstNLTK: the lemma of the answer = the base
form in brackets). The whole sheet text passes `tools/course-audit/language.py`: no unknown words except technical
values (`center`, `none`), the `from` lesson ids and the URL sonaveeb.ee. Both short and long illatives are accepted where
both are correct (kooli / koolisse, kööki / köögisse, raamatukokku / raamatukogusse).

## How it reaches the site

1. **Code release:** merge the PR, then the batched release (main deploys only on `[deploy]` / release.yml). This puts
   the course into „Kursuse tootmine” (admin) and the SVGs under `/textbook-art/kaanded/`. Nothing in Firestore changes.
2. **Records (production write, owner's yes):** create the six `curriculumLessons/kaanded-001…006` documents from
   `kaandedCurriculumRecords(MODULES)`. Use a create-only script run from a clean `origin/main` worktree, which never
   overwrites an existing document (like the b1eksp / b1noor installs). Without the records, „Avalda tund” fails with
   „Õppetundi ei leitud.” (`lessonWorksheets.js publish` needs the lesson document).
3. **Sheets (production write, admin click):** „Kursuse tootmine” → Käänded lauses → „Avalda tund” for each lesson. This
   writes `curriculumLessons/<id>/worksheets/{discover,practice,transfer}` as published sheets.
4. Then the course is in Õppevara → Eesti keel → level „Käänded lauses”. A teacher assigns a sheet by hand. A learner who
   has work in the course sees it in „Minu kursus” (`selfStudyModel.courseChoice`: a course the learner has touched). It
   is never offered by level.

The agent ran none of steps 1–3. They need the owner.
